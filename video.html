Questions

Before you ask

What do you need from me?

A link to your product, and a login to a test account if we record app screens. Photos or footage you already have help, but we do not need them.

Is the voice a real person?

We use high-quality AI voices. You hear samples and pick the voice before we record. Many languages and accents are available.

Where do the shots come from?

For apps, we record your real screens. For products, we use your photos and AI-generated cinematic shots built from them. We do not invent features your product does not have.

Who owns the video?

You do. When the final payment is made, you can use the video on your site, in ads and on social media, with no extra fees.

How do I pay?

50% when you approve the script, 50% before we send the final files. We send an invoice.
