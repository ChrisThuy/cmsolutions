Free diagnostic scorecard

How much of your business is guesswork?

Twelve questions, answered from memory — not how the business is performing, but how much of what you believe is evidenced.

You get a score, four area bars, and your three weakest named — each with the register that settles it.

  • No sign-up
  • Nothing stored
  • Nothing sent anywhere

Premises and pipeline

Planning & VOA

Your three weakest areas

Each one below names the register that settles it. These are public, and in most cases free — you are welcome to go and read them yourself.

What this score is not. Every answer above is your own account of your business, so this measures how well evidenced your decisions are — not how the business is performing. A high score means you are working from facts you can point at. It does not mean the facts are good ones. The registers are what turn an answer into evidence, and this page has not read a single one of them.
