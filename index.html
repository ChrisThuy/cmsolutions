No. 01 companies house

Accounts · Charges · Filing history

Companies House

Nine years of filings tell you more about a business than nine hours of meetings.

Every UK limited company files, and every filing is public, statutory and free to read. Accounts, secured charges, director appointments, share allotments, and the date each one landed. Read as a single document set they show margin drift, borrowing taken against assets, and whether filing discipline is tightening or slipping.

I read yours and your competitors' the same way — same registers, same method, same dates. A charge registered last March is a fact. What it means for your next twelve months is the diagnosis, and it is cited to the filing it came from.

  • Annual accounts
  • Charges (MR01)
  • Filing history
  • PSC register
  • SIC drift

No. 02 planning & voa

Planning portals · Rateable values · Licensing

Planning & VOA

What is being built near you is public twelve to twenty-four months before it opens.

Every local authority publishes every planning application — the applicant, the drawings, the use class, the objections and the decision. A competitor's fit-out, a new anchor tenant, a road closure, a change of use two doors down: all of it is on the portal long before it is on the street.

The Valuation Office holds the rateable value of every commercial property in England and Wales. Set beside the planning record it shows who is expanding, who is quietly shrinking, and whether the rates bill you are paying is defensible against the units either side of you.

No. 03 hm land registry

Title · Price paid · Tenure

Land Registry

Who owns the building, what they paid for it, and when the lease turns.

A title register costs a few pounds and settles arguments that otherwise run for months. Registered owner, tenure, price paid, and every charge secured against the title — for your premises, your landlord's other holdings, or the site a competitor just took.

The useful part is rarely the single title. It is the pattern: a landlord who bought in 2007 and has not refinanced since behaves differently at renewal from one who completed last year on a fresh facility. That is a negotiating position, and it is a matter of public record.

  • Title register
  • Price paid data
  • Leasehold terms
  • Registered charges

No. 04 ons & catchment

Census · Spend · Competitor density

Catchment & demand

How many people can reach your door, and what they have left to spend.

Census 2021 is published down to output area — a few hundred households at a time. Age, tenure, occupation, car access, household composition. Drawn against a real drive-time rather than a circle on a map, it answers whether the customers you are pricing for actually live within reach.

Set that against household spend by category and the density of businesses already serving it, and a site either has room or it does not. This is the difference between "the area feels busy" and a number you can put in front of a lender.
