Free · no account · up to six pages

A website report you can put your name on.

Most business sites are missing three or four small things that cost them visitors — a description search engines have to invent, a link that previews as a bare URL in WhatsApp, images a screen reader cannot describe. None are visible when you look at the page.

Every finding here says what it costs and how to fix it, so the result is something you can hand to a client or straight to a developer. Agencies: add your own name to it and print it.

Checks your home page and follows its navigation, up to six pages. We fetch them the way a search engine would; nothing is stored.

Tell me when this changes

We will check this site every week and email you only when something changes — a new problem, or an old one fixed. A quiet week means no email, because a report that always says the same thing is one nobody reads. One click to stop, no account.

    What it looks at

    Everything here is checked against the page that was actually served, not guessed from the domain.

    Found on the page

    Deterministic checks, no AI involved

    • Title and meta description, including whether they will truncate
    • Main heading, and whether there is exactly one
    • Canonical URL and declared language
    • Link preview tags for Slack, WhatsApp and LinkedIn
    • Structured data, and whether it actually parses
    • Images with no alt text
    • HTTPS, mixed content and security headers
    • Redirect chain and server response time

    Not in this free check

    • Watching for regressions when someone ships a change
    • Rendered-page checks, for sites built in JavaScript
    • Core Web Vitals from real visitors
    • Competitor and keyword context
    • Someone who fixes what it finds
