02

How it works

Two to three weeks
Step 1

The site brief

The address — or the two or three you are choosing between — and the decision you are facing: sign, walk away, or renegotiate.

Step 2

The reading

Catchment drawn, portals read, rating list compared, competitors counted. Two to three weeks depending on how many sites are in play.

Step 3

The report

One document per site: the catchment, the pipeline, the rates position and the density, every figure cited to its source and dated.

Step 4

The walkthrough

An hour on a call. If the record says walk away, the report will have already said it in writing.

03

The price, straight

£1,200 – £2,500

£1,200 for one site with a straightforward catchment; up to £2,500 where several sites are compared or the catchment crosses several authorities. You get the exact figure, fixed, before any work starts.

What this is not. It is not a property valuation and not investment advice — it reads the public record around a site, it does not price the building. And it will not predict footfall; it counts what is there and what is coming, which is what the guesswork was standing in for.
