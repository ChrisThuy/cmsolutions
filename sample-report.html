The findings

One per card, cited beneath
Finding 01

Gross margin has drifted 4.2 points in three years

Turnover held between £1.84m and £1.97m across the last four filed years while cost of sales rose each year. The margin a lender computes from these accounts fell from 31.8% (FY2022) to 27.6% (FY2025). None of the four filings mentions it; together they state it.

Source: full accounts FY2022–FY2025, filed 09 Nov 2022 – 14 Nov 2025 · read 24 Aug 2026
Finding 02

A fixed charge was registered in March 2025

A charge in favour of Midshire Commercial Finance Ltd was registered on 11 Mar 2025 over the unit at 4 Fenwick Trading Estate — the company's only freehold asset. It secures 'all monies due or to become due'. The accounts filed eight months later carry no note connecting the new borrowing to a purpose.

Source: charge code 0482 7719 0003 (MR01), registered 11 Mar 2025 · read 24 Aug 2026
Finding 03

Filing dates are slipping later each year

FY2022 accounts were filed five months after year end; FY2023, seven; FY2024, eight; FY2025 arrived twelve days before the statutory deadline. The direction is the finding: filing discipline usually degrades before anything in the accounts does.

Source: filing history, five filings 2022–2025 · read 24 Aug 2026
Finding 04

Competitor A carries no debt and is accumulating cash

Hollowbeck Interiors Ltd — the nearest of the three comparators — shows cash rising in each of five filed years, no registered charges, and creditors falling. Read beside Findings 01 and 02, the gap in position is widening in Hollowbeck's favour, and they are the party able to wait out a price war.

Source: Hollowbeck Interiors Ltd, full accounts FY2021–FY2025 and clear charges register · read 24 Aug 2026
Finding 05

Competitor B could not be fully read — and that is stated, not padded

Gable End Fit-Out Ltd files micro-entity accounts: no turnover, no profit figure, no meaningful comparison. The report says so and reads what does exist — incorporation date, two director changes in 18 months, and a satisfied charge — rather than dressing thin evidence as thick.

Source: Gable End Fit-Out Ltd, micro-entity accounts FY2023–FY2025; officers register · read 24 Aug 2026
