AI business tools

Tools that finish the job, not demonstrate it.

Most AI products hand you a prompt box and leave the actual work where it was. These are built the other way round: each one takes a specific job a business already has to do, and carries it through to something you can send, file or sign.

Everything here is designed, engineered and supported by CM Solutions. The list is short on purpose — a tool appears once it is genuinely usable, and not before.

Methane Compliance Readiness

For oil & gas operators, importers and exporters

Article 30 required the EU Methane Transparency Database to be running by 5 February 2026; it was not, and launch has been reported for September. From 2027 new import contracts must show measurement and verification equivalent to EU producer standards. Most operators can describe their programme; far fewer can evidence it against each obligation with a date attached. Eight questions gives you the list.

  • EU Methane Regulation, OGMP 2.0 and US GHGRP obligations
  • Every finding cites its source, so it can be checked
  • Ordered by which deadline falls due first
  • Runs in your browser — your compliance posture stays with you

FuelEU Maritime & EU ETS Calculator

For shipowners, charterers and bunker buyers

LNG in a medium-speed Otto engine clears the 2025 GHG intensity limit by 0.13 gCO2eq/MJ and misses 2030 by 3.51 — because Annex I counts fuel that leaves the engine unburned as methane, at 25 times the weight of CO2. Put your own fuel mix in and get the penalty from the regulation's own formula rather than from a model.

  • GHG intensity, compliance balance and the Annex IV penalty in euros
  • EU ETS allowances alongside it, on the same voyages
  • Every factor cited to the Official Journal, including the chain to RED II
  • No invented allowance price and no invented biofuel factors

Document Data Extraction

For anyone retyping numbers off PDFs

Drop in an invoice, receipt, contract or CV and get the fields back as data. The part that matters is what happens when a field is not there: it comes back empty with a reason rather than filled in with something plausible. A wrong total that looks confident is worse than no tool at all, because nobody re-checks a tidy number.

  • Every value quotes the exact text it was read from
  • Flags what it could not verify — cropped scans, totals that do not add up
  • Your own field names, not a fixed template
  • The file is read and discarded — never stored, never logged

How these tools are built

The same three constraints apply to everything in this directory, and they are the reason the list grows slowly.

One job, end to end

Each tool solves a single business job completely rather than being a general assistant pointed at your work. Narrow scope is what makes the output good enough to send to a client.

Structured, checkable output

Output comes back as real structure — sections, tables, figures — not a paragraph that mentions them. Numbers that should reconcile are checked before you ever see them.

Yours to edit

Nothing is generated and locked. You rewrite any part of it, because you are the one whose name goes on the document.
